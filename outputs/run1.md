## Snapshot
Q1 FY27 revenue rose 18% YoY to ₹1,248 crore, profit after tax ₹82 crore, with EBITDA margin slipping to 11.2% (down 170 bps) [S3]
Order book grew to ₹3,900 crore, exports now 21% of revenue, and the new Bharuch plant (30% capacity boost) is slated for commercial production in Q3 FY27 [S3]
Copper prices jumped ~14% in the quarter, pressuring margins; two‑thirds of contracts have price‑variation clauses that reset with a 1‑2 quarter lag [S2]
Receivable days rose to 96, promoter pledging is low at 4.1% of holdings, and the company faces a GST demand of ₹46.3 crore pending appeal [S2] [S8] [S7]

## Bull case
- Robust demand from power transmission and data‑centre projects, with data‑centre cables already 9% of domestic revenue and a plan to double that share in two years [S2]
- Large order book of ₹3,900 crore provides a visible pipeline for near‑term sales [S3]
- New Bharuch plant will add ~30% capacity, enhancing ability to meet growing demand from FY27 onward [S3]
- Low promoter pledge (4.1% of promoter holdings) reduces governance risk for minority shareholders [S8]
- Net debt‑to‑equity of 0.35 indicates modest leverage and capacity to fund capex internally [S2]

## Bear case
- Margins compressed by higher copper input costs; pass‑through to customers may be delayed due to price‑variation clause lag [S2]
- Receivable days increased to 96, signalling potential cash‑flow pressure from large utility customers [S2]
- GST demand order of ₹46.3 crore (including interest and penalty) could affect cash resources if not resolved favorably [S7]
- Revenue figure discrepancy (₹1,428 crore reported by a news article vs ₹1,248 crore in the official filing) raises questions about reporting consistency [S1] [S3]

## Open questions
- When and how fully will the company pass on the higher copper costs to customers, and what impact will that have on FY27 margins? [S2]
- Will the FY27 full‑year revenue guidance of 15‑17% growth be met, given the current order book and capacity additions? [S2]
- What is the likely outcome and timing of the appeal against the GST demand, and could any penalties be larger than stated? [S7]
- Which revenue figure (₹1,248 crore vs ₹1,428 crore) correctly reflects Q1 FY27 performance, and why do the two sources differ? [S1] [S3]
