## Snapshot
Q1 FY27 revenue rose year‑on‑year, with reports ranging from a 18% increase to 1,248 cr (official filing) to a 35% jump to 1,428 cr (news article). [S3] [S1]
EBITDA margin fell 170 basis points to 11.2% as copper prices rose about 14% in the quarter. [S3] [S1]
Order book grew to ₹3,900 cr as of 30 June 2026, up from ₹3,150 cr a year earlier. [S3]
Promoter pledging fell to 4.1% of promoter holdings (June 2026), down from 6.0% in March 2026. [S8]

## Bull case
- Demand from power transmission and data‑centre projects remains strong, with data‑centre cables already 9% of domestic revenue and management targeting a double‑share in two years. [S2]
- The new Bharuch plant, adding roughly 30% to cable capacity, is on track for commercial production in Q3 FY27, supporting future volume growth. [S3]
- Net debt‑to‑equity stands at 0.35×, indicating a low leverage profile. [S2]
- Promoter share pledging has declined sharply, reducing governance risk for minority shareholders. [S8]

## Bear case
- Operating margins contracted due to a 14% rise in copper prices, and the company’s ability to pass on these costs is uncertain. [S1] [S3] [S2]
- Receivable days increased from 78 to 96 days, potentially stressing cash flow despite no defaults reported. [S2]
- A GST demand order of ₹46.3 cr has been raised for prior years’ input‑tax credit claims, posing a contingent liability. [S7]
- Revenue figures for Q1 FY27 are inconsistent across sources, creating uncertainty around the true growth rate. [S1] [S3]

## Open questions
- What is the material impact of the ₹46.3 cr GST demand if the appeal is unsuccessful? [S7]
- Will the company successfully pass higher copper costs to customers within the next two quarters as projected? [S3] [S2]
- Exact timeline and commercial ramp‑up details for the Bharuch plant beyond the Q3 FY27 target. [S3]
- Clarification on the discrepancy between the reported Q1 revenue figures (₹1,248 cr vs ₹1,428 cr). [S1] [S3]
