## Snapshot
Q1 FY27 revenue rose 18% YoY to ₹1,248 crore, profit after tax ₹82 crore, while EBITDA margin fell 170 bps to 11.2%. [S3]
Order book increased to ₹3,900 crore (up from ₹3,150 crore) and the new Bharuch plant, adding ~30% capacity, is slated for commercial production in Q3 FY27. [S3]
Copper prices jumped ~14% in the quarter, compressing margins; management expects to pass on the higher costs to customers over the next two quarters. [S1] [S3]
Receivable days rose to 96 days, driven by two large state utility customers, though no defaults have been reported. [S2]

## Bull case
- A robust order book of ₹3,900 crore signals strong demand and a sizable pipeline for future revenue. [S3]
- The Bharuch plant will boost production capacity by about 30%, enabling the company to meet growing demand, especially from power transmission projects. [S3]
- Data‑centre cable sales now represent ~9% of domestic revenue and management expects this segment to double in two years, offering a high‑growth avenue. [S2]
- Exports now account for 21% of revenue, up from 16% a year earlier, diversifying earnings away from domestic cycles. [S3]

## Bear case
- Margins contracted by 170 bps to 11.2% due to a ~14% rise in copper prices, highlighting raw‑material cost sensitivity. [S1] [S3]
- Receivable days have lengthened to 96 days, indicating slower cash collection from large utility customers. [S2]
- The company faces a GST demand order of ₹46.3 crore for prior‑year input‑tax credit claims, which could become a cash outflow if the appeal fails. [S7]
- Promoter share pledging was reported at 35% in 2024, a level that can raise governance concerns; the latest filing shows a lower 4.1% pledge, creating uncertainty about the current risk. [S4] [S8]

## Open questions
- There is a conflict between S1 (revenue ₹1,428 crore, 35% YoY) and S3 (revenue ₹1,248 crore, 18% YoY) for Q1 FY27; the correct figure needs verification. [S1] [S3]
- The timeline and effectiveness of passing higher copper costs to customers remain uncertain; no concrete pricing guidance is provided. [S3]
- Details on the outcome of the GST demand order appeal and any potential penalties beyond the stated ₹46.3 crore are not disclosed. [S7]
- Current promoter pledge level is unclear given the discrepancy between the 2024 news report (35%) and the 2026 filing (4.1% of promoter holding). [S4] [S8]
