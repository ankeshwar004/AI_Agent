## Snapshot
Q1 FY27 revenue reported at ₹1,248 crore, up 18% YoY, with profit after tax ₹82 crore [S3]
EBITDA margin fell to 11.2% (down 170 basis points) as copper prices rose sharply [S3] [S1]
Order book reached ₹3,900 crore; new Bharuch plant adds about 30% capacity, slated for commercial production in Q3 FY27 [S3]
Promoter pledge low at 4.1% of holdings as of June 2026, contrasting with earlier reports of higher pledging [S8] [S4]

## Bull case
- Demand from power transmission and data‑centre projects remains strong; data‑centre cables now 9% of domestic revenue and management expects to double this share in two years [S2]
- Exports now account for 21% of revenue, supporting growth beyond domestic market [S3]
- Robust order book of ₹3,900 crore provides a visible pipeline for future sales [S3]
- Leverage is moderate with net debt‑to‑equity at 0.35 and most capex funded through internal accruals [S2]

## Bear case
- Margins compressed by higher copper input costs, reflected in a 170‑bp decline in EBITDA margin [S3] [S1]
- Receivable days increased to 96 days, indicating slower cash collection from large utility customers [S2]
- Company faces a GST demand order of ₹46.3 crore; while management expects no material impact, the liability could affect cash flow if upheld [S7]
- Past promoter‑pledge concerns (earlier reports of 35% pledge) may linger in investor perception despite recent lower pledge levels [S4] [S8]

## Open questions
- How effectively and quickly the company can pass on higher copper costs to customers, and the resulting impact on future margins [S3] [S2]
- Whether the Bharuch plant will achieve full commercial output as scheduled and translate capacity addition into revenue growth [S3]
- Outcome of the GST demand order appeal and any potential penalties or interest beyond the stated ₹46.3 crore [S7]
- Full‑year FY27 performance guidance beyond the stated 15‑17% revenue growth range, especially given margin pressure [S2]
